NEXUS

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 15 November 2026

1. Introduction

Nexus is a Discord bot designed to provide server management, community engagement, activity tracking, entertainment and cross-server networking features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nexus collects, how that information is used, when it may be shared and how users can request access to or deletion of their information.

By using Nexus in a Discord server, you acknowledge the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on which Nexus features are enabled, the bot may collect and store the following information:

Discord Account Information

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord usernames and display names.
  • Server IDs and server membership information.
  • Join and leave dates.
  • Invite information, including invite codes and inviter IDs where available.

Activity Information

  • Message counts and activity statistics.
  • Experience points (XP), levels and rankings.
  • Achievement progress, quests and activity streaks.
  • Server and network leaderboard statistics.
  • Boosting duration where available through Discord.

Nexus is designed to track message activity rather than retain the actual contents of ordinary messages for its XP system.

Economy and Game Information

  • Virtual currency balances.
  • Transactions and payment records.
  • Inventory, purchased items and collectibles.
  • Gambling and game results.
  • Quest, achievement and event progress.

Virtual currency and items have no real-world monetary value.

Cross-Server Information

When servers voluntarily establish a connection through Nexus, certain information may be shared between those linked servers. This may include:

  • Invite and referral statistics.
  • Network activity statistics.
  • Shared ban-list records.
  • Information needed to operate cross-server recruitment features.

Cross-server information is only shared through the relevant Nexus network features and according to the linked servers' configuration.

Moderation and Administrative Information

  • Shared ban records.
  • Administrative actions and audit logs.
  • Economy adjustments and transaction logs.
  • Server configuration and feature settings.

3. How We Collect Information

Information is collected automatically through Discord events and interactions with Nexus commands, buttons and other features.

  • Joining or leaving a server may generate an invite-tracking record.
  • Sending a message may update activity and XP statistics.
  • Using an economy command may update your virtual balance.
  • Participating in a linked-server feature may generate network records.

Nexus may also receive information from Discord's API when required to provide its functionality.

4. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

  • Operate Nexus commands and features.
  • Maintain XP, levels, rankings and leaderboards.
  • Manage virtual economies and game progress.
  • Track invitations and server activity.
  • Operate cross-server networking features.
  • Enforce shared bans where enabled.
  • Prevent abuse, exploitation and fraudulent activity.
  • Maintain bot reliability and investigate technical issues.

We do not sell users' personal information.

5. Data Sharing

Nexus does not make all stored information publicly available.

Information may be shared:

  • Between servers that have mutually configured a Nexus network connection, where relevant to the enabled network features.
  • With authorised server administrators through administrative commands and dashboards.
  • With infrastructure or hosting providers necessary to operate the bot, where applicable.
  • Where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect the security of Nexus and its users.

Server administrators may have access to certain statistics and moderation records relating to their own server.

6. Data Storage and Security

Nexus uses an SQLite database to store information required for its features. Database records are designed to persist across bot restarts.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information from unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. However, no digital storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Access to the bot's database and administrative functions should be restricted to authorised operators.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as it is needed to provide Nexus functionality, maintain records or protect the service.

Users may request access to, correction of or deletion of information associated with their Discord account.

Deletion requests may require verification of account ownership. Certain records may be retained where necessary for security, moderation, legal obligations or legitimate operational purposes.

Removing Nexus from a server does not necessarily delete information already stored in its database.
